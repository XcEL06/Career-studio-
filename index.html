Insights

Short, practical guidance on resumes, LinkedIn and professional branding.

  • Lead with value, not duties

    A list of responsibilities says what the job was. A strong resume shows what you contributed and why it mattered.

  • Keep your resume and LinkedIn aligned

    Recruiters compare them. Matching titles, dates and story build trust and avoid confusion.

  • Update when things change

    Revise your profile after a promotion, project or new qualification, not months later when details have faded.

  • Write for people and for systems

    Use clear headings, plain formatting and relevant keywords so both applicant tracking software and readers can follow it.
